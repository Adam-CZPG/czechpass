# Privacy policy

## Who is the controller
The controller is the operator of the CzechPass app: [FILL IN name or company, ID number, address]. Contact: [FILL IN e-mail].

## In short
- The app needs no registration or account.
- Your answers, progress and settings stay stored only on your phone.
- The app contains no advertising or analytics tools and sends nothing about you to our servers.

## What is stored on your phone
- The language, appearance, exam date, daily goal and reminder time you chose.
- Your answers, results, review schedule, badges and unfinished exercises, including texts you write.
This data does not leave your device. The operator has no access to it.

## Microphone and speech recognition
In speaking exercises the app asks for access to the microphone. Speech is turned into text by the speech recognition service on your phone (usually a Google service). Depending on your phone settings, that service may send audio to its provider, who processes it under its own policy. CzechPass does not store recordings and does not send them anywhere itself. You can withdraw microphone access at any time in your phone settings.

## Notifications
The daily reminder is created by the app directly on the phone. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your phone settings.

## When the app uses the internet
- On start it checks through Google Play whether a new version is available.
- Links to official exam websites open in your browser.
Learning also works without an internet connection.

## Purchases
Paid parts are bought through Google Play. The payment is processed by Google; the app receives no payment card details, only the information that the purchase was made.

## Backup
If Android backup is turned on in your phone, the app's data may be part of the backup in your Google account. You manage the backup in your phone settings.

## How to delete the data
Choose "Reset progress" in the app's Settings, or uninstall the app. This removes the data from the phone.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you have the right to information, access, rectification and erasure, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz). Because the data stays on your device, you control it directly.

## Changes to this policy
If we add features that handle personal data differently (for example advertising or AI correction of texts), we will update this policy beforehand and inform you in the app.

The Czech version of this policy is the binding one.
